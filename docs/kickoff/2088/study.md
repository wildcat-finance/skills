## 1. Problem statement, user and proving path

Tabularium's Wildcat V1/V2 adapter decodes `InterestAndFeesAccrued(uint256,uint256,uint256,uint256,uint256,uint256)` and `StateUpdated(uint256,bool)` and gives both the disposition `unsupported-canonical-meaning` with gaps `canonical:market:<signature>` (`plugins/tabularium/scripts/tabularium_lib/wildcat_rows.py:392-396`). The accrual fields and the `isDelinquent` flag never reach `canonical-event-v3`, so a consumer that may not read native logs has to quarantine both events.

Build, for both generations, a native-meaning mapping of the two events with the market as the only party. The user is any consumer of a `canonical-event-v3` Wildcat release. This run stays inside Tabularium's native mapping: no accrual replay, no delinquency projection, no downstream rebuild.

A working prototype means: `wildcat-canonical` maps both events as `primary` rows on a constructed specimen per generation and on both retained releases held in internal custody; each numeric field appears once under its ABI-named kind in its native unit; the two timestamps and `isDelinquent` stay decoded native state; a moved field declaration or malformed log refuses; an unpinned source stays an `unsupported-decode` gap; and `verify` rebuilds every generated byte. The demo path is the Step 4 command set: rebuild and verify both constructed accrual specimens, run `python3 plugins/tabularium/tests/prove_wildcat_accrual.py --candidate abi-named-amounts --criterion retained-conformance --report <fresh path>` with the restored staging trees named by `ALEXANDRIA_WILDCAT_V1_STAGING` and `ALEXANDRIA_WILDCAT_V2_STAGING`, and pass `python3 scripts/run_checks.py --base 1fc995e72cc61267273abd8339a9c1fb2289b068 --scope root --scope tabularium --scope repo-lints --format json`.

Success criteria, each checked by a command:

- The two signatures are absent from `coverage.unsupported_events` on both generations' constructed specimens and on both retained rebuilds; the retained result is one boolean in the resolver report, and it is false, never vacuously true, when a generation maps no row of either action.
- Every `interest-and-fees-accrued` row carries exactly the amounts `scale-factor`, `base-interest-ray`, `delinquency-fee-ray` and `protocol-fees`, in that order, with `base_units` equal to the decoded field; every `state-updated` row carries exactly `scale-factor`. Checked by the Step 2 focused tests and the `semantic-conformance` resolver.
- `fromTimestamp`, `toTimestamp` and `isDelinquent` appear in the mapping record's `decoded` object and in `native_record`, and in no amount or party. Checked by the Step 2 focused tests.
- A pinned definition whose field names or order differ from the rule's declared order refuses; a `StateUpdated` log whose boolean word is neither zero nor one, or a truncated accrual log, refuses; a log from an unpinned source commit stays `unsupported-decode`. Checked by the Step 2 hostile tests.
- Python tuples, both JSON Schemas and the parity oracle agree on the four new branches. Checked by the `schema-parity` resolver.
- The two published Wildcat specimens, the two published batch specimens, the six Aave and Euler releases and schema 2 rebuild byte-identical; `verify` refuses a release whose new rows, amounts, decoded records or coverage were edited after the build. Checked by the Step 2 and Step 3 focused tests and the root suite.

The issue's four unchecked questions, and what the source settles:

- Whether `scaleFactor` is the same quantity in both events: settled as to field and unit. Both emit `MarketState.scaleFactor` in ray. Equality of the two values in one transaction is not asserted; no row joins the two.
- The unit of `protocolFees`: settled. It is a normalized token amount, so underlying-asset base units for the interval's accrual, not a collection or payment. The asset is the market's underlying when the context establishes it, otherwise null with `attribution:interest-and-fees-accrued:asset`.
- How many of each event one transaction emits: settled per call only. One `_getUpdatedState` emits zero, one or two accrual events, and each `_writeState` emits one `StateUpdated`. A transaction can make several market calls, so the per-transaction count stays unknown. The mapping does not depend on it.
- Their order among the other market events: settled per function only. Within `_getUpdatedState` the accrual to expiry precedes the expired-batch events, which precede the accrual to now and the pending batch's payment; where `StateUpdated` falls relative to a function's own event differs by function (V1 `WildcatMarket.sol:158-159` writes state before `Borrow`, `:74-81` emits `Deposit` before the write). The mapping reads no order, so order stays an unchecked data property.

## 4. Design options

Four candidates were drawn. Each maps both events with party `pool`, family `interest-accrual` for the accrual and `pool-state` for the state write, and a declared field order that refuses a moved definition. They differ in where the numeric fields go and in the row envelope.

`abi-named-amounts` puts each numeric field in `amounts` under its kebab-case ABI name: `scale-factor`, `base-interest-ray` and `delinquency-fee-ray` assetless, `protocol-fees` with the underlying asset or null and a gap; `state-updated` carries `scale-factor` alone. Timestamps and `isDelinquent` stay in the decoded record and `native_record`, as `MarketClosed` keeps its timestamp. Its trade: three amounts are ray ratios rather than asset quantities, so a consumer that sums every amount must filter by kind.

`unit-named-amounts` names only the unit: `ray` for the three ray fields and `assets` for `protocolFees`. Its trade: one row carries three `ray` kinds that only their order tells apart, and `assets` makes an accrued fee read like the asset movements `deposit` and `borrow` carry.

`native-state-only` maps both events with empty `amounts` and keeps every field in the decoded record. Its trade: the numeric fields never reach the canonical row, which is what the issue asks to end.

`envelope-native-state` is `abi-named-amounts` plus a `native_state` row field under `schema_version` 4. Its trade: every existing row changes, `verify` would need a fourth read path and every published release a superseding directory.

`.hexaemeron/design-evidence.json` holds the record. Seven selection criteria cover correctness (`numeric-fields-carried`, `kinds-distinct`), time (`field-reads`), space (`new-row-bytes`), compatibility (`schema-envelope-unchanged`, `existing-rows-unchanged`) and recovery (`changed-event-fails-closed`). Each ran `python3 .hexaemeron/design/accrual_probe.py --candidate <id> --criterion <id> --report .hexaemeron/design/reports/<candidate>-<criterion>.json` over source-ordered constructed logs for both generations: two accruals, a deposit pair and two state writes, with and without an asset context, run through the current `map_records`. `unit-named-amounts` fails `numeric-fields-carried` and `kinds-distinct`; `native-state-only` fails `numeric-fields-carried`; `envelope-native-state` fails both compatibility gates. `abi-named-amounts` alone survives, so `unique-frontier` selects it. `python3 <plugin_root>/skills/protasis/scripts/design_evidence.py .hexaemeron/design-evidence.json --transition design-lock` exited 0. The probe models the row shapes; it is not product conformance.

Three conformance cells stay pending with their resolvers: `semantic-conformance` and `schema-parity` resolve through `python3 plugins/tabularium/tests/prove_wildcat_accrual.py` after Step 2 and block `step:3`; `retained-conformance` resolves through the same runner in Step 4 with the staging trees present and blocks `integration`. Each report is a boolean; the retained one also records identifiers and digests only.

The mapping the selected candidate implements, both generations, emitter role `market`:

- `interest-and-fees-accrued`, family `interest-accrual`, party `pool`. Amounts in order: `scale-factor` (`scaleFactor`, ray, assetless), `base-interest-ray` (`baseInterestRay`, ray, assetless), `delinquency-fee-ray` (`delinquencyFeeRay`, ray, assetless), `protocol-fees` (`protocolFees`, underlying base units, asset or null). `fromTimestamp` and `toTimestamp` stay decoded. Gaps: `attribution:interest-and-fees-accrued:actor`, and `attribution:interest-and-fees-accrued:asset` when the asset is unknown. The fees are accrued, not collected or paid.
- `state-updated`, family `pool-state`, party `pool`. Amount: `scale-factor` (`scaleFactor`, ray, assetless). `isDelinquent` stays decoded. Gap: `attribution:state-updated:actor`. The flag does not establish when delinquency began, a penalty or current standing.

The rules are `wildcat-v1.interest-and-fees-accrued.v1`, `wildcat-v1.state-updated.v1` and the two `wildcat-v2` rules formed the same way. The rule set grows from twenty-five to twenty-nine; the mapping-rule enums in both schemas from thirty-six to forty.

## 5. Risk register

```risk-register
native-units | the four numeric ABI fields to their amount kinds | each field appears once under its ABI-named kind with base_units equal to the decoded decimal string and no conversion
ratio-not-asset | ray-scaled scale factor and rate fields to the amount list | the three ray kinds stay assetless and no row or document reads them as an asset quantity
fee-not-collected | protocolFees to the protocol-fees kind | the asset is the underlying or null with the asset gap and no prose reads the fee as collected or paid
native-state | the timestamps and isDelinquent to the row envelope | they stay in the decoded record and native_record and never become an amount, party or row field
delinquency-boundary | isDelinquent to downstream claims | no row or document reads the flag as default, delinquency entry, a penalty or current standing
cause-not-inferred | an accrual or state write to other events in its transaction | no party, limitation or join names another event as its cause and the two events are never joined
field-order | the pinned declaration to the rule's declared field order | a moved, renamed or retyped field refuses and an unpinned source stays an unsupported-decode gap
abi-bounds | untrusted accrual and state-update log fields to the decoder | topic count, word width, boolean words and arity refuse malformed data
schema-closure | the four new branches to both JSON Schemas and the parity oracle | Python tuples, schema branches and rule enums agree and reject a fifth shape
existing-rows | the twenty-five released rules to their bytes | the four published Wildcat specimens, the six Aave and Euler releases and schema 2 rebuild byte-identical
pinned-counts | tests that enumerate rules, branches or nullable-asset kinds | every count and kind list named in item 12 moves with the change and no pin is deleted to pass
promise-repin | EVOLUTION.md digest to the promise-machine fixtures | the fixture, coverage map and obligation-gate files are re-pinned in the step that adds the generation row
```

## 6. Glossary seeds

`ray`: an integer scaled by 10^27, so `RAY` reads as one.
`scale factor`: `MarketState.scaleFactor`, the ray multiplier from scaled claims to normalized amounts.
`base interest ray`: the lender interest accrued over one accrual interval, in ray.
`delinquency fee ray`: the delinquency penalty accrued over the penalty seconds of one interval, in ray.
`protocol fees`: the fee accrued on one interval's interest, in underlying base units; not collected.
`accrual interval`: the seconds from `fromTimestamp` to `toTimestamp` of one accrual event.
`state write`: one `_writeState` call, which emits one `StateUpdated`.
`isDelinquent`: the stored flag set when required liquidity exceeds total assets at a state write.
`native state`: a decoded field kept in the mapping record and `native_record` rather than in an amount or party.
`attribution gap`: a named absent party or asset on a decoded primary row.
