## 1. Problem statement, user and proving path

Tabularium's Wildcat V1/V2 adapter decodes `WithdrawalBatchCreated(uint256)`, `WithdrawalBatchPayment(uint256,uint256,uint256)`, `WithdrawalBatchExpired(uint256,uint256,uint256,uint256)` and `WithdrawalBatchClosed(uint256)` and then gives each the disposition `unsupported-canonical-meaning`. The scaled-token burn that the market emits beside every batch payment comes out as a `transfer` row nothing joins. A consumer that may not read native logs itself has to quarantine every such transaction.

Build, for both generations, a native-meaning mapping of the four events and a same-transaction join between each `WithdrawalBatchPayment` and its burn. The user is any consumer of a `canonical-event-v3` Wildcat release, first among them Probitas under #1926, which owns the batch-status family downstream. This run stays inside Tabularium's native mapping: no queue replay, no unpaid-status projection, no downstream rebuild.

A working prototype means: `wildcat-canonical` maps all four events as `primary` rows on a constructed specimen per generation and on both retained releases held in custody; every payment row carries its burn's selector and a `join-inference` record; the burn row carries the payment's selector back; a burn with no adjacent payment, a payment with no adjacent burn, a value mismatch or a reordered pair leaves a named gap and no join; and `verify` rebuilds every generated byte. The demo path is the Step 4 command set: rebuild and verify both constructed batch specimens, run `python3 plugins/tabularium/tests/prove_wildcat_batches.py --candidate adjacent-burn --criterion retained-conformance --report <fresh path>` with the two retained staging trees named by `ALEXANDRIA_WILDCAT_V1_STAGING` and `ALEXANDRIA_WILDCAT_V2_STAGING`, and pass `python3 scripts/run_checks.py --base 50d71a2e8c1777f8eb26db32dd8adeadb34063bd --scope root --scope tabularium --scope repo-lints --format json`.

Success criteria, each checked by a command:

- The four signatures are absent from `coverage.unsupported_events` on both generations' constructed specimens and on both retained rebuilds; the retained result is a boolean in the resolver report.
- Every `withdrawal-batch-payment` row on those inputs has one `join` whose rule is `adjacent-market-burn`; the joined `transfer` row carries no `attribution:transfer:unclaimed-burn` gap.
- Hostile fixtures with a reordered burn, a changed burn value, a burn without a payment, two burns before one payment and a payment without a burn produce the named gap and no join; a malformed batch log refuses.
- The two published Wildcat specimens, the six Aave and Euler releases and schema 2 rebuild byte-identical.
- `verify` refuses a release whose events, selectors, join record or coverage were edited after the build.

The deployed source settles which path emits what. In both generations `_applyWithdrawalBatchPayment` is the only function that emits `Transfer(market, 0x0, normalizedAmountPaid)`, and it emits `WithdrawalBatchPayment(expiry, scaledAmountBurned, normalizedAmountPaid)` as the very next log (V1 `src/market/WildcatMarketBase.sol` lines 604 to 633; V2 lines 650 to 680). It is reached from `_getUpdatedState` for an expired pending batch and for the pending batch after accrual, from `queueWithdrawal` after `WithdrawalQueued`, from `_processUnpaidWithdrawalBatch` under `repayAndProcessUnpaidWithdrawalBatches`, and in V2 also from `closeMarket`. `WithdrawalBatchCreated` is emitted when a queue call finds no pending batch (V1 `WildcatMarketWithdrawals.sol` line 107, V2 line 96) and by V2 `closeMarket` for the one-second batch it opens (V2 `WildcatMarket.sol` line 274). `WithdrawalBatchExpired` is emitted by `_processExpiredWithdrawalBatch` with the batch's cumulative totals and by V2 `closeMarket`. `WithdrawalBatchClosed` is emitted when a batch is fully burned at expiry, in `_processUnpaidWithdrawalBatch` and unconditionally by V2 `closeMarket`. V1 emits the queue `Transfer` before `WithdrawalBatchCreated`; V2 emits `WithdrawalBatchCreated` first. Neither the created, expired nor closed event has a burn of its own. One transaction can emit several payments, including two for the same expiry when `queueWithdrawal` pays the pending batch during the state update and again after the request, and `repayAndProcessUnpaidWithdrawalBatches` pays up to `maxBatches` batches. A lender may call `transfer(0x0, amount)`, which emits a transfer from the lender, not from the market; the market itself emits no other transfer to the zero address. The V1 `_blockAccount` path moves claims to an escrow address, not to zero.

## 4. Design options

Four candidates were drawn. Each maps the four events the same way; they differ in how a payment finds its burn and in the row envelope.

`adjacent-burn` joins a `WithdrawalBatchPayment` to the log at `log_index - 1` when that log is `Transfer(address,address,uint256)` from the same market emitter, `from` is the market, `to` is the zero address, the transaction hash is the same and `value` equals `normalizedAmountPaid`. The six conditions are the emission order of `_applyWithdrawalBatchPayment`. The payment row appends the burn's selector and a `join-inference` record with rule `adjacent-market-burn`; the burn's `transfer` row appends the payment's selector and the same record. Any failed condition leaves `attribution:withdrawal-batch-payment:burn` on the payment and `attribution:transfer:unclaimed-burn` on a market-to-zero transfer nothing claims. Created, expired and closed carry no join because the source gives them no burn. Its trade: it reads log order, which the source fixes and which a provider cannot reorder without changing `logIndex`.

`unique-value-burn` copies the escrow key: same market and transaction, `value` equal to `normalizedAmountPaid`, unique in both directions. Its trade: two payments in one transaction can carry equal amounts, which the source permits, and then neither joins; a reordered burn still joins.

`all-batch-events` reads the issue literally and joins every batch event to the nearest earlier burn. Its trade: one burn is claimed by its payment and again by the expired or closed event that follows, so a single transfer counts under two rows.

`envelope-batch-field` is `adjacent-burn` plus a `batch` field and `schema_version` 4 so the expiry becomes a row field. Its trade: every existing row changes, `verify` would need a fourth read path and every published release a superseding directory.

`.hexaemeron/design-evidence.json` holds the record. Eight selection criteria cover correctness (`source-pairing-determinism`, `single-claim`, `retained-join-closure`), time (`pair-comparisons`), space (`retained-candidates`), compatibility (`schema-envelope-unchanged`, `existing-rows-unchanged`) and recovery (`missing-or-ambiguous-stays-gap`). The seven constructed criteria ran `python3 .hexaemeron/design/batch_join_probe.py` over source-ordered specimen transactions, including two payments with equal amounts and the five hostile shapes, and over the current `map_records` output for the published fixtures. `retained-join-closure` ran `python3 .hexaemeron/design/retained_join_probe.py`, which rebuilds both retained raw releases and canonical releases in a private temporary tree and reports one boolean with the raw release identifiers and canonical digests; it prints and stores no count. `unique-value-burn` fails determinism and recovery; `all-batch-events` fails determinism, single claim and recovery; `envelope-batch-field` fails both compatibility gates. `adjacent-burn` alone survives, so `unique-frontier` selects it. The probes are models of the pairing rule, not product conformance.

Three conformance cells stay pending with their resolvers: `semantic-conformance` and `schema-parity` resolve through `python3 plugins/tabularium/tests/prove_wildcat_batches.py` after Step 2 and block `step:3`; `retained-conformance` resolves through the same runner after Step 4 with the staging trees present and blocks `integration`. Each report is a boolean; the retained one also records digests only.

## 5. Risk register

```risk-register
join-adjacency | the six-condition burn key to the source emission order | a payment joins only the log at index minus one that is a market-to-zero transfer with equal value in the same transaction
single-claim | one burn to many batch rows | no burn selector appears under more than one join and expired and closed rows carry no join
unclaimed-burn | a market-to-zero transfer with no adjacent payment | the transfer stays a primary row with the unclaimed-burn gap and no join is guessed
payment-without-burn | a payment whose preceding log is absent, reordered or has another value | the payment row records the burn gap and no companion selector
reserved-not-received | normalizedAmountPaid to the reserved-assets unit | no row or document reads a payment as lender receipt, spendable cash or execution
cause-not-inferred | a batch event to the action sharing its transaction | no party or limitation names a repay, queue, execute or close as the batch event's cause
expiry-identity | the uint32 expiry to the row envelope | the expiry is preserved in the decoded mapping record and native record and never coerced into a party or amount
empty-amounts | created and closed rows to the amount rules | only the two batch rows and market-closed admit an empty list and every other action keeps its cardinality
schema-closure | the four actions to the JSON Schema branches and enums | Python tuples, schema branches, mapping-rule enums and the parity oracle agree and reject a fifth shape
existing-rows | the seventeen released rules to their bytes | both published Wildcat specimens, the six Aave and Euler releases and schema 2 rebuild byte-identical
verify-rebuild | an edited release to offline verification | changed events, selectors, join records, dispositions or coverage are refused by the rebuild
abi-bounds | untrusted batch log fields to the decoder | topic count, word width and arity refuse malformed data for the four signatures
partial-output | release creation and interruption | fresh staging plus atomic completion leave no half-written release that verifies
private-custody | retained captures to public bytes | no count, address, transaction, block or amount from the retained releases enters a committed file or report
report-truth | the two registered runners to Fiat evidence | exact argv, in-process tests, actual exits and a boolean-only retained report
prose-boundary | mapping prose to downstream claims | no settlement, standing, identity, accounting or chain-proof claim
```

Prose around the block: the join is the one new inference in this run and the first two ids bound it; every other id guards a boundary #1378 already drew.

## 6. Glossary seeds

`batch`: the set of withdrawal requests sharing one expiry timestamp on one market.
`expiry`: the `uint32` timestamp that identifies a batch; native state, not a party.
`pending batch`: the batch still accepting requests; one per market at a time.
`unpaid batch`: an expired batch whose burned claims are below its total, held in the market's FIFO.
`scaled claims`: the market's accounting units; `scaledTotalAmount` and `scaledAmountBurned` are scaled claims.
`burn`: `Transfer(market, 0x0, normalizedAmountPaid)` emitted by `_applyWithdrawalBatchPayment`.
`reserved assets`: `normalizedAmountPaid`, underlying units set aside in the market for a batch; not received, spendable or executed.
`adjacent join`: the six-condition pairing of a payment with the log before it.
`unclaimed burn`: a market-to-zero transfer no adjacent payment claims.
`attribution gap`: a named absent party or companion on a decoded primary row.
