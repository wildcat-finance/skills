# Dokimasia evolution ledger

Policy: [../../../hexaemeron/skills/VERSIONING.md](../../../hexaemeron/skills/VERSIONING.md)

- Current version: `dokimasia-v3.1.0`
- Frontier status: `open`
- Frontier revision: `browser-observed-dispositions`
- Current frontier: Dokimasia admits a confirmed entry only when it names the person who confirmed it and, where a rule was applied, a row in the set's `rules` table stating that rule and who stated it, and its coverage and scrutiny records report confirmations by person and by rule. The pinned scrutiny of `wildcat-app-v2` at `bb9685fb` still closes at 202 over 261 with `covered` at zero, now attributed to one person under one stated rule. Every one of those entries was drafted from the workbook; none records anything observed in the running application.
- Next Fiat job: Drive a browser over the reviewed UAT workbook's paths so that a Playwright-driven pass over a pinned application checkout, and not a hand-edited file, drafts the dispositions a person then confirms. Today every disposition is drafted from the workbook, so the record can say who confirmed an item and under what rule but not that anything was seen to happen. Accepted when a Playwright-driven walk over a pinned application checkout produces a drafted disposition set that `reconcile` admits with zero attributed entries, when every drafted entry names the route, action or guard it exercised and the oracle it observed, when the walk is pinned by application commit and browser version and a second walk of the same pin agrees with the first, when a person's confirmation still carries `confirmed_by` and, where rule-based, `rule`, and when a test proves the crawler cannot write either attribution field.
