- Frontier status: `open`

- Current frontier: The curation graph and the deterministic release ship. Submissions, findings, occurrences, remediation attempts and verifications are separate records joined by many-to-many edges; a severity outside the policy taxonomy is quarantined rather than mapped; a duplicate cluster arrives from the policy and keeps every submission; and a release rebuilds byte-for-byte or refuses. The Elenchus and Synkrisis projections are declared boundaries that refuse by name.
- Next Fiat job: Ship the read-only consumer projections runbook step 3 owes: an Elenchus analogue view that cannot carry a past verdict into a present case, and a Synkrisis cohort projection that emits only the admitted observation schema with its denominator and exclusions intact. Accepted when a past guarded verdict cannot reach an Elenchus answer, an undeclared producer or a missing cohort field is refused, restricted material crosses neither adapter, and the deterministic rebuild report exists at the path the design record names.
